My Commercial Growth System

Understand the engine. Fix the constraints. Scale what works.

Sustainable growth happens when Sales and Marketing work toward the same goal — more revenue with more profit. I see the commercial journey as one connected system, not a collection of departmental activities.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Diagnose

Understand the system before changing it.

  • Customer problem and target persona.
  • Product value — not just features.
  • Funnel leakage, conversion and lead quality.
  • CRM evidence, people, process and economics.
02 — FIX

Remove friction

Fix the constraint that is limiting commercial performance.

  • Measure the funnel and find the weakest stage.
  • Improve lead quality and qualification.
  • Align Sales + Marketing around one revenue objective.
  • Address capability, process and execution gaps.
03 — SCALE

Build the system

Turn successful interventions into repeatable growth.

  • Scale channels, products and processes that work.
  • Build repeatable acquisition and conversion systems.
  • Use continuous experimentation and market feedback.
  • Develop teams that perform without constant firefighting.

Leadership

People move the numbers.

My leadership approach combines commercial accountability with coaching, clarity and cross-functional alignment. The goal is not simply to manage a target — it is to build a team and operating system capable of delivering it repeatedly.

01

Diagnose before you judge.

Separate skill, confidence, process, lead quality, motivation and role-fit problems before deciding the response.

02

Build confidence and ownership.

Give people voice, responsibility and coaching — then step back as their capability grows.

03

Revenue is a shared responsibility.

Marketing and Sales work toward one commercial objective with shared funnel definitions and accountability.

04

Build Systems, Not Heroics.

Sustainable growth should not depend on one person working harder every month. It should become an operating system.

  • Repeatable acquisition and conversion systems.
  • Continuous improvement and learning loops.
  • Teams that can perform without constant firefighting.
